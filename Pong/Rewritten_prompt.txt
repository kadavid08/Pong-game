Make a simple Pong game in C# using a Windows Forms App in Visual Studio. The player controls the left paddle with W and S, and a simple AI controls the right paddle by following the ball. The ball should bounce off the top and bottom walls and both paddles. When someone misses, the other side gets a point and the ball returns to the middle. Show the score at the top, and stop the game with a winner message when someone reaches 5 points. Add one Start/Restart button that starts a new game and resets both scores. Keep both paddles inside the playing area. Use a dark navy background, a cyan player paddle, an orange AI paddle, a yellow ball, and a thin centre line. Keep the style clean and arcade-like. Use a Timer, basic variables, if statements and short methods. Keep the code simple, readable and suitable for a beginner, with a few helpful comments and no advanced features.

Make a simple Pong game in C# using Windows Forms in Visual Studio. Control the left paddle with W and S, and let a simple AI control the right paddle by following the ball. The ball should bounce off both paddles and the top and bottom walls. When someone misses, the other side gets a point and the ball resets to the middle. Show the score at the top. First to 5 points wins, and a message should show the winner. Add a Start/Restart button that resets the game. Keep both paddles inside the playing area.

Use a dark navy background, a cyan player paddle, an orange AI paddle, a yellow ball and a thin centre line. Make it look clean and cool. Use a Timer, basic variables, if statements and short methods. Keep the code easy for a beginner to understand, with a few helpful comments and no unnecessary features.
